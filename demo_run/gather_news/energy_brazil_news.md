# The Future of Energy in Brazil - Latest News Summary

## 1. Renewables Hit Record Levels, Solar Dominates Expansion
**Sources:** [GNPW Group](https://www.gnpw.com.br/en/energy-transition/sustainable-energy-in-2026-brazil-at-the-center-of-the-global-energy-transition/), [Industrial Info](https://www.industrialinfo.com/iirenergy/industry-news/article/brazil-boosts-power-output-in-2025-with-more-expected-in-2026--352073), [IEA](https://www.iea.org/countries/brazil)

- Brazil closed 2024-2025 with 88.2% renewable electricity, and the IEA projects renewables will reach 95% of the electricity mix by 2026.
- In 2025, Brazil added 7,403 MW of new capacity (63 solar plants totaling 2,815 MW, 43 wind farms totaling 1,825 MW). For 2026, ANEEL expects 9,142 MW — a 23.4% increase.
- Nearly 12 GW of new solar capacity was added in 2025 alone; solar now accounts for almost a quarter of Brazil's entire electricity matrix.
- PV projects (utility-scale and distributed) will represent nearly 70% of all capacity additions going forward.
- Renewables reached a historic milestone of 50% of total primary energy supply, the highest since 1990.

## 2. Energy Storage Becomes Central to the Next Phase
**Sources:** [Odyssey Energy Solutions](https://odysseyenergysolutions.com/news-insights/brazils-energy-transition-enters-a-new-phase-and-storage-moves-to-the-center), [BNamericas](https://www.bnamericas.com/en/analysis/renewable-expansion-tests-the-limits-of-brazils-power-grid)

- With the approval of MP 1.304 and the government's plan for the first battery-only auction, energy storage is moving from experimental to strategic priority.
- Battery energy storage projections show potential for 7.2 GW of installed capacity by 2040, with investments potentially exceeding R$ 22 billion by 2030.
- The shift from a system historically dependent on large hydroelectric plants to one with high solar/wind shares requires flexible storage and dispatch solutions.
- Grid stabilization and decongestion remain key challenges as rapid renewable expansion strains the transmission infrastructure.

## 3. Green Hydrogen: R$ 64 Billion in Investments and Petrobras Pilot Plant
**Sources:** [Renewables Now](https://renewablesnow.com/news/petrobras-to-power-up-2-mw-green-hydrogen-pilot-plant-in-2026-871421/), [BioEnergy Times](https://bioenergytimes.com/petrobras-expects-to-commission-its-inaugural-pilot-green-hydrogen-plant-in-2026/), [Click Petroleo e Gas](https://en.clickpetroleoegas.com.br/Investments-in-green-hydrogen-in-Brazil-could-exceed-R$64-billion-in-2026--with-direct-effects-on-industry--energy-transition--infrastructure--and-global-competitiveness.-HL1402/)

- Petrobras will commission its inaugural 2-MW green hydrogen pilot plant in Q1 2026, powered by the Alto Rodrigues solar farm (BRL 90 million investment).
- Investments in green hydrogen in Brazil projected to exceed R$ 64 billion by 2026.
- Projects with Final Investment Decisions planned for 2026 total 6.15 GW of electrolysis capacity, positioning Brazil among the world's largest green hydrogen pipelines.
- Brazil established the legal framework through Law No. 14,948/2024 (National Hydrogen Policy).
- Petrobras increased its energy transition budget by 42% to US$16.3 billion (15% of total CAPEX for 2025-2029).

## 4. Offshore Wind: Ambitious Plans Face Regulatory Delays
**Sources:** [Brazil Energy Insight](https://brazilenergyinsight.com/2025/10/08/brazil-risks-postponing-the-first-offshore-wind-auction-in-2026/), [ICLG](https://iclg.com/practice-areas/renewable-energy-laws-and-regulations/brazil), [Global Energy Monitor](https://www.gem.wiki/Brazil_Atlantic_Coast_Offshore_Wind_Development)

- Law No. 15,097/2025 was signed by President Lula in January 2025 establishing the regulatory framework for offshore wind generation.
- Brazil plans to install more offshore wind capacity than the rest of the world has built combined, despite currently having zero offshore wind farms.
- However, regulatory guidelines may only be presented in H1 2026, risking postponement of the first offshore wind auction originally planned for 2026.
- A Working Group has 270 days to submit guidelines to CNPE for implementing the legal framework.

## 5. Massive Transmission Infrastructure Investment Cycle
**Sources:** [Power Technology](https://www.power-technology.com/features/renewables-boom-aftermath-brazils-energy-sector-tackles-excess-power-supply-challenges/), [BloombergNEF](https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-energy/brazil-transition-factbook-2025-the-numbers-behind-the-ambition/), [GNPW Group](https://www.gnpw.com.br/en/energy-transition/sustainable-energy-in-2026-brazil-at-the-center-of-the-global-energy-transition/)

- Brazil is entering one of its largest investment cycles in power transmission.
- Two transmission auctions planned for 2026: one expected to attract R$ 5.7 billion for 888 km of new lines, and another for R$ 20+ billion covering 3,500+ km.
- Total transmission expansion investments of up to R$ 120 billion planned through 2030.
- Industrial Info is tracking more than 1,900 renewable power and energy storage projects across Brazil, totaling over US$ 629 billion in investment.

## 6. Biofuels Remain Strong
**Sources:** [IEA](https://www.iea.org/countries/brazil), [IRENA](https://www.irena.org/Publications/2025/Nov/The-energy-transition-in-Brazil)

- Ethanol supply set to average 660 kb/d in 2026, up 90 kb/d from 2020.
- Brazil's pre-salt oil extraction registers ~40% fewer emissions per barrel than the world average.
- Biomass is consolidated as a firm and complementary power source alongside solar and wind.
